💊 Rosheta — UI/UX Case Study

📎 Project Name

Rosheta — Medication Discovery, Prescription Review & Pharmacy Delivery Platform

📌 Project Overview

Rosheta is a proposed mobile platform that helps users find hard-to-find medications, compare nearby pharmacies, and manage the journey from prescription upload to delivery.

Users can compare pharmacies by medication availability, distance, price, delivery fees, and estimated arrival time, helping them choose the option that best meets their needs.

The platform focuses on three core features:

- Medication Search & Pharmacy Comparison:** Find medications and compare available pharmacy options.
- Smart Prescription Scanner & Pharmacist Review:** Extract prescription details, flag unclear information, and require pharmacist review before order confirmation.
- Drug Interaction Checker:** Provide informational alerts about potential interactions and guide users to seek advice from a doctor or pharmacist.

This UI/UX case study documents the design process, including user research, competitor analysis, personas, information architecture, user journeys, wireframes, interface design, prototyping, and usability testing.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Understand the challenges users face when searching for unavailable medications.
- Explore pharmacists’ needs when reviewing prescriptions and clarifying medication details.
- Conduct market research and competitor analysis to identify opportunities for improvement.
- Develop user personas, empathy maps, and journeys based on research findings.
- Design an intuitive experience for medication search and pharmacy comparison.
- Clearly communicate medication availability, prices, delivery fees, and estimated arrival times.
- Create a prescription-scanning flow that highlights uncertainty and includes pharmacist review before order confirmation.
- Design understandable drug-interaction alerts with clear guidance to seek professional advice.
- Simplify ordering and delivery tracking.
- Evaluate and refine the prototype through usability testing.
